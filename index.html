Classified Dossier · Guardian

THE INDIGO TOWER INCIDENT

02:14 AM. Meridian Corp's Indigo Tower — ten floors of offices, labs, and the server core that keeps the whole campus running. An intrusion alert just tripped on the ground floor, and it's climbing.

You're the on-call Cyber Guardian. Whoever — or whatever — is inside the building has already been through HR's password files, Finance's invoices, and the exec suite. Every floor they've touched left a trace: a clue pointing to where they went next. Your job is to follow that trail up through all ten floors and lock the threat out of the Server Room before it locks everyone else out first.

Security is still active on every floor. Guards patrol on schedule, terminals are still logged in, and one wrong move — a guard's flashlight, a wrong password, a wrong floor in the elevator — ends the mission on the spot. Move carefully. Read every clue twice.

Guardian · Arcade Mission

Mission Instructions

Mission

An endless descent. Each floor is the same six zones — Main Gate, Checkpoint, Office Floor, NOC, Data Center Corridor, and the Server Room. Reach the server rack to clear the floor, and the tower immediately rebuilds itself harder: +5% enemy speed and +10% more threats every single floor, forever. There is no timer. See how deep you can get.

Integrity, not a clock

You have an integrity bar instead of a countdown. Every hazard hit costs a chunk of it and the run ends at zero — so a drone collision is no longer just lost points, it's a resource you can't get back. Clearing floors and Repair pickups patch some of it back.

Controls

  • Move: Arrow Keys or WASD
  • EMP pulse: X or F (or the ⚡ button) — disables every drone in radius for a few seconds and feeds your combo
  • Perk draft: click a card, or press 1–3
  • Touch d-pad appears automatically on tablets

Chain your combo

The multiplier now rewards speed. Every pickup and every EMP stun refreshes a short chain window; keep chaining and the multiplier climbs 1.5× → 2× → 2.5× → 3× → 4× → 5×. Let the window lapse and it decays on its own. Take damage and it drops straight back to 1×.

Draft a perk every 3 floors

Every third floor cleared, the run pauses and offers three randomized perks — Silent Footsteps, Wider EMP Radius, Extra Health, Fleet Runner, Combo Keeper, Hardened Shell, Scavenger and more. Pick one; it stays for the rest of the run and they stack.

Guardian · Story Campaign

Ten Floors of Indigo Tower

How it works

Each floor is a self-contained challenge — a Dangerous-Dave-style run/jump/climb level, a security quiz, or a treasure hunt for a hidden clue. Solve it, and a riddle points you toward the next correct floor.

Controls

  • Platform floors: Arrow Keys or A/D to run, Up/W/Space to jump, Up/Down on a ladder to climb
  • X or F fires a short-range stun charge; once you've picked up a guard's gun, G fires it too — unlimited ammo, and it can permanently take down guards, not just stun them
  • Quiz & treasure floors: click or tab + Enter
  • Touch d-pad appears automatically on tablets

Bonus content

Every floor also hides a scatter of 🪙 coins (pure score), one 🛡 shield pickup (a few seconds of guard/hazard immunity), one or two small patrol bots you can stomp from above for a bounce (touch them any other way and it's a hazard, same as a guard), and a slow bonus platform to ride.

One mistake ends it

  • A guard catches you → mission over
  • You fall into a gap with no floor → mission over
  • A wrong quiz answer → mission over
  • The wrong treasure pick → mission over
  • The wrong floor in the elevator → mission over

Scoring

On platform floors, every trophy you collect banks points and the exit door stays locked until you've grabbed them all. Every floor you clear banks points too — later floors are worth more. Reach Floor 10 and secure the server rack to complete the mission. Your time and floors cleared are tracked on the Story leaderboard.

Guardian · First-Person Campaign

FPS Mode: Lobby Breach

How it works

A first-person, raycasted walkthrough of all 10 floors of Indigo Tower, one after another. Every floor is a real multi-room layout connected by doors that slide open as you approach. Find every badge, avoid the guard(s) on patrol, and reach the elevator alcove to advance to the next floor. Floor 10 ends in a real fight — a Sentinel guarding the exit that has to be stunned down before you can leave.

Three kinds of floor

  • Floors 1–3 — Maintenance Tunnels: cramped, pillared corridors. Guards hold tight loops by the doorways and won't see you coming down a hall — they step out as you round the corner. Close range, fast reactions.
  • Floors 4–7 — Datacenter: wide arenas full of server racks. Snipers hold the room: they paint a laser sight on you, hold the lock for a beat, then fire hard. Get a rack between you and the lock — breaking line of sight cancels the shot outright.
  • Floors 8–10 — Rooftop Extraction: an open arena and a countdown. The exit stays sealed until extraction is ready, while waves of attackers spawn in on a cadence. Explosive barrels are scattered around — shoot one to clear a crowd, but don't be standing next to it when you do.

Controls

  • Move: W/S or Up/Down — walk forward/back
  • Look: click the view to steer with the mouse, or A/D and Left/Right to turn
  • Strafe: A/D or Left/Right once the mouse is steering
  • Stun: X or F (or the lightning button, or click once the mouse is steering) — fire a stun charge straight ahead
  • Interact: E (or the on-screen button) — use a nearby terminal
  • Touch controls appear automatically on tablets

Health, not instant death

  • A guard's touch costs you health (with a brief breather before it can hit you again) — run out and the mission ends
  • Collect all badges and clear any guard blocking the exit, then reach the elevator → advance to the next floor

Ammo, keycards & hidden rooms

You start with limited stun charges — treat them as ammo. Firing one paralyzes the guard briefly if it's directly ahead of you and in range. Every floor hides a hacking terminal (its own quiz — a correct answer is worth a bonus charge), a keycard that unlocks one of the floor's doors, and a couple of tucked-away caches worth extra ammo or a health boost — explore past the obvious route to find them.
